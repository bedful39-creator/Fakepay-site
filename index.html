Frequently Asked Questions

Before you download

Install, versions, and exactly what PayMod does and doesn't touch — before you grab the jar.

What is PayMod for DonutSMP? +

PayMod is a free, client-side Minecraft mod built for DonutSMP. It changes only what YOUR game renders — your wallet balance, your stats, and outgoing payments — replacing them with virtual numbers you choose. The server, other players, and every real leaderboard are completely unaffected.

What exactly does it change visually? +

Every number you own: wallet balances in menus and on your HUD, stat lines like kills, deaths, streaks, playtime and rank — plus full payment flows with confirmation prompts, transaction chat lines, and animated balance tickers. All of it renders inside your own client.

Is the fake money real? Can I spend it? +

No — and that's the point. The money only exists on your screen. It never leaves your account, never enters anyone else's, and never touches DonutSMP's economy. Think of it as a visual layer for clips and screenshots, not an exploit.

Which Minecraft versions and mod loaders work? +

One universal .jar covers Minecraft Java 1.20.1, 1.20.2, 1.20.4, 1.21, and 1.21.4+ on both Fabric and Forge — the same versions DonutSMP players run today.

How do I install it? +

Hit Download Mod, drop the universal .jar into your .minecraft/mods folder, and launch your Fabric or Forge profile. Join DonutSMP and your wallet is ready to rewrite — no config file needed to start.
